# Completed Work Records

Move a record here when its declared objective is complete, its required review or verification has reached a terminal outcome, and no write remains under the claim.

Keep the record. It is the durable account of who owned the work, which bytes or regions were in scope, what evidence was produced, and where the outcome landed. Do not rewrite a completed record to make later work look as though it belonged to the earlier claim.

If the subject needs more work later, open a new record in `Work/Active/` with a new exact scope. A completed record is history, not continuing authority.
