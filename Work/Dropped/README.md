# Dropped Work Records

Move a record here when its declared work will not continue. Before moving it, state why it stopped, what was produced or learned, and whether any partial artifact remains provisional or unusable.

Keep the record. A dropped direction is part of the project's evidence and prevents a later session from unknowingly paying for the same dead end again.

Dropping a record grants no permission to publish, reuse, or treat partial work as approved. If the direction is revived, open a new record in `Work/Active/` with a new exact scope and current evidence.
